Admission planning

Understand school fees before you make a choice

A fair comparison needs the full cost for your child’s class and the year they will join. Use these questions when speaking with an admissions office.

Published by Sanskar Public School. Exact fees are not listed here because they can vary by class and service; request the current written schedule.

Ask for a complete, class-wise quotation

  • Tuition and payment schedule: What is due monthly, quarterly or annually for this grade?
  • One-time charges: Are registration, admission or security amounts payable, and are any refundable?
  • Transport: Is your route available, and what does it cost for your pickup point?
  • Activities and materials: Ask which costs are included and which are separate.
  • Availability: Confirm the class, session, documents and next admission step.
