Three questions to ask

Looking beyond a search result

When searching for the best school in Mathura, define what “best” means for your family: teaching that suits your child, manageable travel, clear communication and a fee plan you understand. A campus visit usually reveals more than a headline or a star rating.

Is the board verified?

Check the school’s affiliation and classes on the official board portal. Ask about subject choices for the grade your child will enter.

How does the day work?

Ask to see classrooms, activities and sports spaces. Talk through homework, student support and parent communication.

What is the full cost?

Request a written, current class-wise schedule. Compare tuition, one-time charges, activities and transport together.
